Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

PitchLive is a live football scores service available at pitchlive-web.onrender.com and in the PitchLive mobile app. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information, and PitchLive shows no ads.

1. Information we collect

When you use PitchLive without an account

  • Nothing that identifies you is stored by us. You can read live scores, match pages, tables and leagues without signing in.
  • On your own device: the website keeps two small settings in your browser's local storage: matches you star on the scores page, and whether you have already seen the welcome page. The mobile app keeps the clubs you follow on your phone. This information never leaves your device.
  • Technical data: like every website, our hosting provider receives your IP address, browser type and the pages you request when you connect. We use your IP address briefly to protect the service from abuse (for example, to limit repeated sign-in attempts). We do not use it to identify or track you.

When you create an account or sign in

  • Email address and display name. If you sign up with email, you give us these directly. If you continue with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and name. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Google data.
  • Account identifier. Our sign-in provider gives each account a unique ID, which we store to link you to your PitchLive account.
  • Password. Passwords are handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication. PitchLive never sees or stores your password.
  • Clubs you follow, so we can show you their matches and alerts.
  • Live chat messages you post on match pages, together with your display name and the time you posted. Chat messages are visible to other people viewing that match.
  • A sign-in cookie (pl_session) that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary for the account features and is not used for advertising or tracking.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show the clubs you follow and the alerts you asked for.
  • To display your chat messages under your display name.
  • To send account emails you request or need, such as email verification and password reset links.
  • To keep PitchLive working, secure and free of abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

3. Who processes your information

We use a small number of trusted providers to run PitchLive. Each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): sign-up, sign-in, Google sign-in, verification and password reset emails.
  • Render (Render Services, Inc.): hosts the website and our servers, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Neon (Neon, Inc.): hosts our database, in Frankfurt, Germany, where account details, followed clubs and chat messages are stored.
  • Content providers: when you open PitchLive, your browser loads club crests and league logos from ESPN's image servers, country flags from flagcdn.com, fonts from Google Fonts, and some league and club details from TheSportsDB. These providers receive your IP address and browser details as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account details, followed clubs and chat messages are kept for as long as you have an account.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days, or immediately when you sign out.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account details, followed clubs and chat messages from our database within 30 days.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your information, or object to how we use it. To delete your account or make any request, email pitchlive16@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will respond within 30 days. You can also sign out at any time, and clear the settings stored on your device through your browser or by uninstalling the app.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Security

All connections to PitchLive are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are handled by Firebase and never stored by us, sign-in cookies cannot be read by scripts, and our servers only accept requests from PitchLive's own website.

7. Children

PitchLive is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell signed-in users in the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: pitchlive16@gmail.com.
